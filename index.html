PRACTICE AREAS

Three core areas,
clearly set out.

Litigation, civil claims and dispute resolution, each handled from first advice to final outcome.

01

Litigation

We appear and argue for clients in civil proceedings at the High Court and on appeal. The work runs from the first letter of demand through pleadings, trial, judgment and enforcement, with the client briefed at every stage rather than at the end.

02

Civil Matters

Claims founded on contract, land and property, debt and tenancy. Land and title disputes make up a large share of what chambers handles in Ibadan, and those files are treated as urgent from the day they arrive.

03

Mediation & ADR

Structured negotiation and mediation for parties who would rather settle than litigate, including settlement agreements that are enforceable. Where court is already running, ADR is used alongside it to narrow the issues.

Dispute resolution

When a dispute
needs a different path.

Mediation and arbitration as alternative approaches to dispute resolution, with litigation where neither will serve.

Mediation

A structured, facilitated process in which parties work towards a mutually acceptable resolution with the support of a neutral third party.

Arbitration

A private adjudicative process where a neutral arbitrator considers the parties’ positions and issues a binding determination.

Litigation

Where settlement is not possible, the matter is taken through the courts, from the first letter of demand to judgment and enforcement.

COMMON QUESTIONS

The questions
clients ask first.

The questions new clients ask before the first meeting, answered plainly.

What should I bring to a first consultation?

Every document that touches the matter: agreements, receipts, letters, survey plans, court processes already served on you, and any correspondence with the other side. A file is only as strong as the paper behind it, and the first meeting is where we establish what exists.

How are your fees set?

Either a fixed sum agreed in writing before work starts, or a professional fee based on the applicable scale. Litigation is usually a brief fee plus appearance fees. Nothing is billed that was not discussed first.

Do you take matters outside Ibadan?

Yes. Chambers is based in Ibadan, and appears in courts within Oyo State and in other states where instructed, briefing counsel on the ground where that is more economical for the client.

How quickly will I hear back?

Enquiries sent through this site are answered within one working day. Urgent matters, particularly injunctions and matters with a limitation deadline, are picked up the same day.

Is what I tell you confidential?

Yes, without exception. Everything disclosed to a legal practitioner in the course of a professional engagement is privileged, whether or not the engagement proceeds.
