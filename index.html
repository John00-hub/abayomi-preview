THE FIRM

A CONSIDERED
APPROACH TO LEGAL WORK.

O. Abayomi & Associates is a full-service legal practice based in Sango, Ibadan, providing legal practice, intellectual property consultancy, mediation, arbitration, corporate secretarial and notarial services.

The practice is structured to serve individuals, businesses and organisations requiring clear, structured legal guidance across contentious and non-contentious matters. The firm’s professional scope spans general legal services, intellectual property advisory, structured dispute resolution, and corporate governance support delivered with discretion and attention to detail.

DISPUTE RESOLUTION

WHEN A DISPUTE
NEEDS A DIFFERENT PATH.

The practice provides mediation and arbitration services as alternative approaches to dispute resolution.

MEDIATION

A structured, facilitated process in which parties work towards a mutually acceptable resolution with the support of a neutral third party.

ARBITRATION

A private adjudicative process where a neutral arbitrator considers the parties’ positions and issues a binding determination.
