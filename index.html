PRACTICE AREAS

Five areas,
clearly set out.

Litigation, real estate and construction, corporate and commercial practice, arbitration and mediation, and notary services. Each is conducted from first advice to final outcome.

01

Litigation

The firm appears and argues for clients in criminal and civil proceedings, and in matrimonial causes. Instructions are conducted from the first letter of demand through pleadings, trial, judgment and enforcement, with the client briefed at each stage rather than only at the end.

  • Criminal litigation
  • Civil litigation
  • Matrimonial matters
02

Real Estate & Construction Law

Land, title and the built environment. The firm handles acquisitions, perfection of title and Governor’s consent, leases and tenancy, and the drafting and negotiation of construction and development agreements. Land and title disputes form a substantial part of the firm’s civil practice, and such files are treated as urgent upon receipt.

03

Arbitration & Mediation

Structured negotiation, mediation and arbitration for parties who prefer settlement to litigation. Terms are reduced to agreements capable of enforcement, and where proceedings are already before the court, alternative dispute resolution is pursued alongside them to narrow the issues in dispute.

04

Notary Services

Notarial attestation and certification of documents for use within Nigeria and abroad, including powers of attorney, affidavits, statutory declarations and instruments of protest. Appointments are attended to at the firm’s office and, where the document requires it, at the client’s address.

05

Corporate & Commercial Practice

The firm advises companies, founders and investors on the legal side of doing business in Nigeria, from incorporation to the agreements that govern a company’s operations. Work is handled end to end, and where a filing or approval lies with a regulator, the firm carries it through rather than handing it back to the client.

  • Business registration
  • Regulatory advisory
  • Compliance

Dispute resolution

When a dispute
needs a different path.

The firm acts in arbitration and mediation as alternatives to litigation, and in litigation where neither will serve.

Mediation

A facilitated process in which the parties work towards a mutually acceptable resolution before a neutral third party.

Arbitration

A private process in which a neutral arbitrator hears the parties and issues an award binding upon them.

Litigation

Where settlement is not possible, the matter proceeds through the courts, from the first letter of demand to judgment and enforcement.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently asked
questions.

What clients most often ask before instructing the firm, answered plainly.

What should I bring to a first consultation?

Every document that touches the matter: agreements, receipts, letters, survey plans, court processes, correspondence and any identification. Bring originals and copies. The more complete the file at the first meeting, the more definite the advice we can give on the day.

How is your fee structured?

Fees are agreed in writing before work begins, and depend on the nature and complexity of the instruction. Most contentious matters are charged in stages: pleadings, interlocutory applications and trial. Non-contentious work is quoted per transaction. Disbursements such as filing fees and travel are billed separately and are always disclosed in advance.

Will I deal with one lawyer or several?

One. Every file has a counsel with conduct of it, and that counsel is the person you call. Where a matter needs a second pair of hands for research or drafting, the file does not change hands. The named counsel remains responsible for it throughout.

Do you act in mediation and arbitration, or only in court?

Both. The firm appears in the superior courts in criminal, civil and matrimonial causes, and also acts for clients in arbitration and mediation. In many commercial and land disputes the quickest and least expensive route to a resolution is a negotiated one, and we will tell you plainly when a matter is better settled than tried.

Do you take instructions from outside Ibadan?

Yes. The firm is based in Ibadan and appears in courts throughout Oyo State and in other states where the matter requires it. Instructions from clients outside Nigeria are accepted, and meetings can be held by telephone or video where travel is not practical.

How soon will I hear back after enquiring?

Enquiries made during office hours are usually answered the same working day. If your matter is urgent, such as an injunction, an arrest or a deadline about to expire, call the office directly rather than using the form, and say that it is urgent.
