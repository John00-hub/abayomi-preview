O. Abayomi & Associates is a law practice in Ibadan, Nigeria, advising individuals, businesses and institutions on contentious and non-contentious matters. Dispute resolution

When a dispute
needs a different path.

Mediation and arbitration as alternative approaches to dispute resolution, with litigation where neither will serve.

Mediation

A structured, facilitated process in which parties work towards a mutually acceptable resolution with the support of a neutral third party.

Arbitration

A private adjudicative process where a neutral arbitrator considers the parties’ positions and issues a binding determination.

Litigation

Where settlement is not possible, the matter is taken through the courts, from the first letter of demand to judgment and enforcement.

COMMON QUESTIONS

The questions
clients ask first.

Short answers to what new clients usually ask before making contact.

How do I contact the firm?

Call +234 703 939 5019, message the firm on WhatsApp, or use the enquiry form on this page. The firm will explain what it needs and whether it can assist.

Should I send documents with my first message?

No. Please do not send documents, identity papers or detailed allegations through this website. If the firm can act, it will explain how to share what is needed.

What should I bring to a first meeting?

If a meeting is arranged, bring the papers that touch the matter: agreements, receipts, letters, survey plans, any court processes served on you, and correspondence with the other side.

Does the firm take matters outside Ibadan?

Chambers is based in Ibadan. The firm can discuss matters outside Oyo State where it is able to act, and will say plainly if it cannot.

How are fees discussed?

Fees and scope are discussed and agreed before work begins. Depending on the matter this may be a fixed sum or a fee based on the applicable professional scale; litigation may involve a brief fee and appearance fees.

Is what I tell the firm confidential?

Communications with a legal practitioner are generally confidential, and professional privilege can attach once the firm is instructed. That is not a reason to send sensitive material through a website: please keep a first enquiry brief, and the firm will explain a suitable route for anything confidential.
