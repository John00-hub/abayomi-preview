Privacy notice

How this website handles your information.

Last updated · 22 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

This website belongs to O. Abayomi & Associates, a law practice at Emi Abata Legal Heritage Building, Opposite Gateway Baptist Church, Iso Pako Road, Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria. The firm is responsible for information sent to it through this site.

2. What the enquiry form collects

The form asks for your name, an optional phone number and email address, the service you are enquiring about, and a short message. It also contains a hidden field used only to deter automated submissions.

Please do not send documents, identity papers, detailed allegations or other sensitive material through the form.

3. What happens when you press “Prepare enquiry”

This website does not store your enquiry. The form prepares a message in your browser and opens WhatsApp so that you can send it yourself. The message is delivered only when you press send inside WhatsApp, and WhatsApp then handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

If a direct delivery service is connected in future, this notice will be updated before that route is used.

4. Calls and WhatsApp messages

If you call or message the firm, the firm receives your number and whatever you choose to say. Those details are used to respond to your enquiry and to manage any work that follows.

5. Why the information is used

  • To read and respond to your enquiry.
  • To decide whether the firm can act, and to avoid conflicts of interest.
  • To keep the ordinary records a law practice is expected to keep.
  • To meet legal, professional and regulatory obligations.

6. How long it is kept

Enquiries are kept only as long as they are needed for the purpose above, or as long as the firm is required to keep them by law or professional rules. Ask the firm if you want to know how this applies to your own message.

7. Cookies and local storage

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser stores one preference — whether you chose the light or dark appearance — so the site can remember it. Clearing your browser storage removes it.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask to see the personal data the firm holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted where the law allows. You may also object to certain uses. Requests can be made by telephone on +234 703 939 5019.

9. Changes to this notice

If the way the site handles information changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
